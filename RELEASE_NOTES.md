# Principia Release Notes

Paste-ready text for App Store Connect. `npm run release` prints the section for
the version it just uploaded, so you can copy it straight into App Store Connect.

Each release gets one section, newest first. Name the actual content: specific,
countable additions show subscribers that "all future content included" is real.

---

## 1.0.5

### What's New (App Store)
40 new geometry practice problems in Tier 4 — angles and parallel lines, congruence and similarity, the Pythagorean theorem, coordinate geometry, proof, circles, surface area, volume, transformations, and the triangle inequality now have roughly twice the practice.

New: a daily practice goal and streak on the Progress tab. Answer 10 questions a day to meet your goal, and keep your streak going one day at a time. Everything stays on your device.

### What to Test (TestFlight)
- Progress tab: a "Today" card shows questions answered today out of 10 and your day streak.
- Answer a few practice questions (in a concept or in Mixed Review), return to Progress, and confirm the count went up.
- Tier 4: open any geometry concept and check the Practice tab has 6–7 questions.

### Review notes (App Review)
This update adds practice content and an on-device daily goal and streak. No new permissions, accounts, or data collection. Subscription products are unchanged.

## 1.0.4

### What's New (App Store)
42 new practice problems across Tier 3 — linear equations, systems, exponents, functions, and domain and range now have roughly twice the practice. New problems feed Mixed Review automatically. Also: clearer terms and privacy policy.

### What to Test (TestFlight)
- Tier 3 concepts have 6–7 practice questions each.
- Mixed Review on the Progress tab draws from the new questions.

### Review notes (App Review)
This update adds practice content and corrects our Terms and Privacy Policy, which previously mentioned Android and Google Play. Principia is iOS only. Subscription products are unchanged. No login is required to review the app.
